SWESS

Site-Wide Echo Spreading System

SWESS is Hulahoot's platform-wide content distribution system for Founding Industry Partners.

  • Founding Industry Partners publish through SWESS using the annual post credits included with their partnership package.
  • SWESS distributes partnership content platform-wide across Hulahoot.
  • SWESS does not operate on a price-per-click workflow.
  • SWESS does not operate on a price-per-impression workflow.

Partnership Post Pricing

Each partnership package includes an annual allocation of publishing credits.
